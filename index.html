Our Story

Founded in 2005, the school was established to bring quality, values-driven education to the young people of North Lakhimpur and the surrounding region. What began as a small campus has grown into a thriving community of learners, educators and families who share a single belief — that every child deserves the chance to shine.

We balance a rigorous academic programme with sport, art, music and community service, so our students leave us not only with strong results but with strong character.

🎯

Our Mission

To deliver a well-rounded education that fosters curiosity, confidence and compassion in every learner.

🔭

Our Vision

To be a leading school in Assam, known for producing thoughtful, skilled and responsible citizens.

Academics

A curriculum built to inspire

From foundational middle-school years to senior secondary specialisation, our academic programme is structured, supportive and ambitious.

Classes VI – XII

Our senior secondary wing prepares students thoroughly for their board examinations, while our middle school builds the strong foundations in language, mathematics, science and the humanities that make later success possible.

  • Well-structured streams in Science, Commerce and Humanities
  • Experienced subject-specialist teachers and small learning groups
  • Smart classrooms and digital learning resources
  • Regular assessments, remedial support and career guidance
  • Consistent, above-average board examination results

Admissions

Begin your child's journey

A warm welcome awaits. Joining Rev. William Ward Senior Secondary School is simple and transparent.

How to apply

  1. 1
    Enquire

    Call, email or visit the school office to collect details.

  2. 2
    Apply

    Fill in the admission form and submit the required documents.

  3. 3
    Interaction

    A friendly meeting and, for some classes, an assessment.

  4. 4
    Enrol

    Complete the formalities and welcome to the family!

Documents you'll need

  • Birth certificate
  • Previous school report card
  • Transfer certificate (for new admissions)
  • Two passport-size photographs
